How do I reserve a scooter?
+
Fill out the form below with your rental dates, delivery address, and a bit about the rider. We'll confirm availability, answer any questions, and finalize everything before you're charged. No surprises.
Is delivery really included?
+
Yes — completely. Delivery, white-glove setup, the charger, and pickup at the end are all included within our core service area. We schedule around your availability, including evenings and weekends.
How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?
+
Within 24 hours — and usually much sooner. We're owner-operated and take every request personally. For urgent needs, call or text us directly at (303) 616-2468.
Do I need to put down a deposit or go through a credit check?
+
No credit check required. A security deposit is not normally required, though one may be requested in certain situations. We'll always be upfront with you about any requirements before you commit to anything.
Will the scooter fit in my car?
+
Most of our travel scooters disassemble and fit in SUVs, minivans, and hatchbacks. Smaller sedans may not have enough trunk space. Just tell us your vehicle type when you reach out and we'll help you figure it out — we even show you how to take it apart at delivery.
Can I pick up the scooter instead of having it delivered?
+
Yes, customer pickup may be available by arrangement. Keep in mind the rental price doesn't automatically change for pickup — the scooter is still cleaned, charged, inspected, and supported throughout your rental.
What if something goes wrong during my rental?
+
Call or text us right away and we'll troubleshoot with you. If the scooter becomes unusable due to a mechanical issue — not caused by damage or misuse — we'll work to provide a replacement, arrange a repair, or give a fair credit or refund for the unused portion of your rental.
Can I rent a scooter for someone else?
+
Absolutely — this is one of the most common situations we see. Many customers rent for a parent, grandparent, or someone recovering from surgery. The person arranging the rental is responsible for payment, safe use, and return of the equipment.
Where can the scooter be used?
+
Our scooters work great on smooth, stable surfaces — sidewalks, paved paths, shopping centers, airports, hotels, parks, and more. They're not designed for stairs, steep hills, sand, mud, deep gravel, or rough terrain.
